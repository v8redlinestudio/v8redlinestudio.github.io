2. Purposes of Processing

  • Create and authenticate guest accounts and save, restore and synchronize game progress.
  • Provide game content, codex, missions, attendance, seasons, mail and rewards.
  • Confirm purchases, validate receipts, deliver products, restore entitlements and prevent duplicate grants.
  • Monitor service stability, diagnose errors, improve quality and prepare usage statistics.
  • Detect and respond to data manipulation, duplicate rewards, payment abuse and other unauthorized activity.
  • Provide rewarded and interstitial ads, manage privacy choices, measure advertising and verify rewards.
  • Respond to inquiries, rights requests, complaints and disputes.

7. Your Rights and How to Exercise Them

  1. Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or suspension of processing, or withdraw consent.
  2. Submit requests to v8redlinestudio@gmail.com. The Operator may verify your identity and any legal limitations before responding.
  3. Uninstalling the app may remove only local device data. Guest account and game data stored on a server may remain; send a separate email request to delete server data.
  4. Where required, use “Ad Privacy Choices” in the app settings to review or change advertising choices.
  5. A parent or legal guardian may exercise rights on behalf of a child where provided by applicable law.

8. Deletion

Personal information is deleted without undue delay after the applicable retention period or purpose ends. Electronic files are deleted using methods intended to prevent practical restoration or reproduction. Paper records, if any, are shredded or incinerated. Information retained under law is segregated and not used for unrelated purposes.

9. Security Measures

The Operator applies reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including least-privilege access, protection of authentication information, encryption in transit, duplicate transaction controls, access and error log review, security updates, data minimization and service-provider management.

10. Automatic Collection and Advertising Identifiers

The service and third-party SDKs may use device identifiers and similar technologies for service delivery, diagnostics, usage statistics and advertising. Google Mobile Ads may process IP address, product interactions, diagnostic data, advertising ID, app set ID and similar device or account identifiers.

You can reset or delete an advertising identifier or limit personalized advertising through Android or iOS privacy and advertising settings. In regions where advertising consent is required, choices can be changed using “Ad Privacy Choices” inside the service.

References: Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure and Google Privacy Policy.

11. Children's Privacy

The service is not directed primarily to children under 14. The Operator does not knowingly process information that requires parental consent without obtaining that consent. If the Operator learns that such information was processed without valid consent, it will take reasonable steps to verify the circumstances and delete the information without undue delay.
