Privacy policy

V8Redline operates 625 Defence (625Defence) and processes information for game accounts, saved progress and support as described below. Our privacy contact is v8redline.studio@gmail.com.

Effective date: 2026-10-19

Current service scope

The app is in Google Play internal testing. Paid-product sales and automated account-deletion intake are disabled. Email support for account or privacy requests. We update this policy as the service and features change.

2. Included Google services

The app uses Google Play Games, Firebase Authentication and Firebase base SDKs. The current internal-test app also includes Firebase Analytics. Under default settings, Analytics may process app-instance identifiers, available Android advertising identifiers, coarse region derived from masked IP addresses, app lifecycle/screen/session and purchase events. The absence of in-game advertisements does not mean that this SDK processing is absent.

Play Games also processes gamer information and analytics/diagnostics for service reliability. This website adds no analytics scripts, advertising or tracking cookies. The Hosting provider may process network information needed to serve requests.

Further information: Firebase Analytics disclosure, Play Games disclosure, and Firebase privacy.

3. Providers and processing locations

Google Firebase/Google Cloud provide authentication and online storage/processing. The game database and API use the Seoul region, but Firebase Authentication processes information in the United States. Other Google services' processing locations depend on their service and settings; we do not claim Korea-only storage. Email inquiries are handled through the relevant email provider.

We do not sell personal information. Providers may process information needed for service delivery, support or lawful requests. For Google's own accounts and services, also read the Google privacy policy.

4. Retention and deletion

Active account/progress information is retained for service use; deletion requests are handled after ownership checks. We explain applicable exceptions and backup scope when handling a request. Current project policy uses five years for applicable transaction/refund/grant/consumption/probability evidence, three years for complaint/dispute/deletion receipts and 60 days for restoration-prevention deletion markers, separated from active profiles with restricted access.

Google services' access/analytics information is also subject to their retention/deletion settings. Specific Analytics retention and final operational log/backup settings for internal testing remain items to verify and finalize before public launch. This notice does not claim verified automatic deletion or verified configuration of every retention setting.

Device language, volume, accessibility and some recovery data may remain locally. Uninstalling the app or clearing device data does not delete a server account. Deleting a Google account is also separate from deleting this game account.

5. Security and your requests

Online transmission uses HTTPS. Authentication and permissions restrict server access/account handling. We minimize unnecessary information in inquiries and avoid exposing authentication/purchase tokens in public screens or ordinary support requests.

Email v8redline.studio@gmail.com to request access, correction, erasure, processing restrictions or game-account deletion. We explain any required ownership checks and handling. The account-deletion page is available without reinstalling the app.

6. Updates

We update this page and its effective date when data practices, features, providers or retention rules change. Significant changes are communicated in the game or official support pages.
