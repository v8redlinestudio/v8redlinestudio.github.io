Request account deletion

Email us to request deletion of your 625 Defence game account and associated data. This does not request deletion of your entire Google account.

Email an account deletion request

Use the subject 625 Defence account deletion request and include:

  1. Your intent to delete the 625 Defence account
  2. The in-game support ID, if known
  3. An email address for our reply, which may be the address sending the request

You may contact us even if you cannot open the app or do not know your support ID. We explain any required ownership checks in our reply. Do not send passwords, verification codes or full purchase tokens.

Sending an inquiry does not mean deletion is complete.

Automated deletion intake is disabled during current internal testing. After receiving your email and checking linked-account ownership and deletion scope, we explain handling and expected timing. A support ID alone is not sufficient. Completion is communicated separately.

Data covered

Deletion covers the active game profile, platform links, campaign progress, roster/cards, game wallet and account-related mail/rewards. Completed deletion is irreversible. Device language, volume and accessibility settings are separate from the game account.

Separately retained records

Where applicable, project policy retains transaction, refund, grant, consumption and probability evidence for five years; complaint/dispute and deletion receipts for three years with restricted access. The policy retains a deletion marker for 60 days to prevent account resurrection after restoration. We explain the applicable retained records and backup scope when handling a request.

Paid-product sales are currently disabled. Only records needed for legitimate retention or disputes remain; keeping an active game account is not a substitute for deletion.
